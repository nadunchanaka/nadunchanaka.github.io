## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an airy, poetic, and refined aesthetic tailored for wedding suites, boutique matrimonial planners, and celebratory digital experiences. Inspired by blossoming springtime lilacs set against fresh botanical greens, the visual narrative balances organic warmth with timeless editorial grace.

The core design philosophy merges **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile / Glassmorphic** layers:
- Generous breathing room and expansive whitespace allow imagery, sentimental vows, and event timelines to be read effortlessly.
- Fine hairline dividers and gentle lavender-tinted surfaces replace aggressive drop shadows, preserving an ethereal, tranquil atmosphere.
- Typography celebrates classic romance with high-contrast serif headers paired with legible, poised sans-serif body typography.
- Emotional resonance evokes romance, bespoke hospitality, calmness, and celebratory joy.

## Colors

The color palette directly draws from the delicate petals and lush garden stems of the reference imagery:
- **Primary (`#846D9B` - Muted Lilac Violet):** A rich, grounded violet that balances romance with high accessibility. Used for key interactive targets, prominent editorial headings, and signature accents.
- **Secondary (`#637A5B` - Herbal Leaf Green):** A fresh, organic sage-olive tone pulled from the lilac foliage. Provides natural grounding for secondary call-to-actions, status badges, badges, and RSVP markers.
- **Tertiary (`#BBA8CD` - Soft Lilac Bloom):** A powdery, luminous pastel violet used for soft highlights, decorative tags, focused states, and delicate active states.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8FC` - Lilac Whisper Cream):** A warm, tinted alabaster base that eliminates cold digital white in favor of soft paper-like warmth.
- **Deep Text Neutral (`#2B2433` - Deep Plum Slate):** High-contrast, near-black violet for primary body text, ensuring effortless readability across screens.

## Typography

The pairing blends the classic, editorial sophistication of **Playfair Display** with the clean, modern warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans**:
- Headlines utilize the romantic transitions and calligraphic serifs of Playfair Display, often styled with italic flourishes for names, vows, and dates.
- Body, forms, navigation, and operational UI components rely on Plus Jakarta Sans for pristine clarity across all devices.
- All uppercase metadata labels (e.g., date stamps, ceremony locations, dress codes) employ tracked letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to mimic bespoke letterpress stationery.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (`> 1024px`) with 1.5rem gutters and generous 3rem outer margins, providing spacious framing reminiscent of high-end wedding albums.

On mobile devices (`< 768px`), the grid collapses into a 4-column system with 1rem gutters and 1.25rem safe canvas padding. Generous vertical rhythms are maintained through `space-xl` and `space-2xl` section gaps, guaranteeing that visual elements never crowd delicate portraits or text blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained without heavy or artificial drop shadows, favoring light, atmospheric depth:
- **Tonal Layers:** Elevation is defined primarily through gentle surface contrast—placing pure matte white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) against soft lilac-cream tinted backdrops (`#FAF8FC`).
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Subtle 1px borders in diluted lilac tones (`rgba(132, 109, 155, 0.12)`) establish structure and separate cards without visual clutter.
- **Lilac Ambient Glow:** For floating dialogs, dropdowns, and elevated action buttons, use an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with violet: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(132, 109, 155, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(132, 109, 155, 0.04)`.
- **Translucent Overlays:** Modals and bottom sheets use subtle backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with an 85% opacity lavender-tinted background.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded geometry (level 2). Cards, modules, and inputs feature gentle `0.5rem` corners to mirror tailored invitation cards, while interactive pills and tags use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`) or full circular pill radii to convey softness and approachability.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Solid `#846D9B` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` roundedness, with subtle hover lift and color transition to `#755E8C`.
  - *Secondary:* Transparent background with a crisp 1px border in `#637A5B`, text in `#637A5B`, shifting to a 10% herbal green wash on hover.
  - *Text/Ghost:* No border, muted lilac text with an understated underline that animates on hover.

- **Cards:**
  - Crafted to resemble fine cotton paper: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border in `rgba(132, 109, 155, 0.12)`, `0.5rem` to `1rem` corner radius, and generous internal padding (`space-lg`).
  - Used for registry highlights, wedding party bios, itinerary stops, and RSVP cards.

- **Chips & Tags:**
  - Compact rounded pills (`1.5rem` radius) with `rgba(187, 168, 205, 0.2)` background and `#846D9B` text for event tags (e.g., "Rehearsal Dinner", "Cocktail Attire", "Vegetarian").

- **Input Fields & Text Areas:**
  - Clean border style with transparent or white base, bordered with `rgba(132, 109, 155, 0.24)`. Focus state transitions seamlessly into `#846D9B` with a soft lilac focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(187, 168, 205, 0.3)`).

- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Rounded geometry with `#846D9B` fill upon selection, featuring a crisp white tick or inner pip. Unselected states use subtle herbal-gray outlines.

- **Decorative Elements (Wedding-Specific):**
  - *Botanical Hairline Dividers:* 1px wide rules carrying an optional centered floral or monogram emblem.
  - *Timeline Trackers:* Vertical 1.5px lines in `#BBA8CD` connecting schedule milestones marked by soft lilac nodes.
